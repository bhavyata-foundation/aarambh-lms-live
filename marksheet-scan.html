Scan a marksheet

Reads the handwritten B, P and A marks from a photo of a marksheet. Nothing is saved: you check every mark and correct anything wrong.

1The photo

Photograph only the part with the marks, flat and in good light. The whole sheet is fine too — you choose the area next.

2The marks area

Drag the four round handles onto the four corners of the box of marks. The photo is straightened from these corners, so a tilted photo is fine.

3The grid

Leave rows and columns empty to find them from the printed lines. If some lines are missing or faint, type the number of children (rows) and marks per child (columns).

4Check and correct

Each box shows the handwriting from the photo and the mark read. Boxes with a yellow border need a look. Tap B, P, A or – (no mark) to correct.
